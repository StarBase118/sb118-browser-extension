# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.**

The extension handles your StarBase 118 sign-in session, so a bug in it can have real
consequences for other members. Report it privately instead:

- **Preferred:** [open a private security advisory](https://github.com/StarBase118/sb118-browser-extension/security/advisories/new)
  on this repository. Only the maintainers can see it.
- **Alternative:** email **website@starbase118.net**.

You will get an acknowledgement within a few days. Please give us a chance to ship a fix
before describing the problem publicly.

## What is in scope

Anything in this repository, and anything about how the extension talks to StarBase 118:

- A way to see data belonging to another member
- A way for a non-staff member to reach staff-only results or links
- Anything that exposes, stores, or transmits your session cookie
- A page or site that can make the extension act on your behalf

## What is out of scope

- Bugs in StarBase 118's own websites that do not involve the extension — report those
  through the normal **Report an issue** link
- The staff section appearing in the popup: that is a display convenience, and every
  staff-only page enforces its own permissions server-side
- Reports generated by an automated scanner with no working proof

## How the extension handles your session

Worth knowing before you report:

- **It never asks for, stores, or fills a password.** Sign-in happens on StarBase 118's own
  Authentik page, in a normal browser tab.
- It makes credentialed requests to StarBase 118 hosts only. Your session cookie is sent by
  the browser to those hosts, exactly as it would be if you typed the address yourself.
- Nothing is sent anywhere else. There is no analytics, no telemetry, and no third-party
  service involved.
- What you type in the search box goes to the SB118 systems being searched, and nowhere
  else.
